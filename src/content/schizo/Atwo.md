it made me weak. small. it caged my spirit and locked it away, 
when it should have been held wild and untamed. it tried to
control me, to civilise me.

it could never last.

i did, in a way, appreciate the thought. to think that i, me?
could be civil? could be held as a *good* and *reasonable* man?
i liked the idea, but ultimately it was shallow and sickening.
